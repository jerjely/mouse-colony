Mouse Colony: privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Mouse Colony is a desktop application used by members of the Völgyi lab (Szentágothai Research Centre, University of Pécs) to manage the lab's mouse breeding colony. It runs on the user's own computer. There is no Mouse Colony server: the developers receive no data from the app.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to read and edit Google Sheets and to read Google Drive files. It uses this only to open the one colony spreadsheet whose link you enter in the app (or receive from a colleague): it downloads that spreadsheet, writes your changes to it, and adds a line to its change log. It does not open, list, change or delete any other file in your Google account. It reads your e-mail address only to show which account is signed in and to record it in the colony sheet's change log.

Where data is stored

  • The colony records are in the Google Sheet owned by the lab and visible to the people it is shared with.
  • The app keeps local backup copies of the colony sheet on your computer.
  • Your Google sign-in (an access token) and settings are stored only on your computer, in the app's settings file. They are not sent anywhere except to Google when the app accesses the sheet.

Cage-card photos

If you choose to use photo reading, the photos you select are sent to Google's Gemini API, with your own Gemini API key, to read the text on the cage cards. Google's terms for the Gemini API apply to those photos.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the features described above.

Mouse Colony's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Use Shared database → Sign out in the app to delete the sign-in from your computer, or revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app removes its settings; the colony sheet itself stays in Google Drive.
