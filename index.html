Mouse Colony

An in-house desktop tool of the Völgyi lab, Szentágothai Research Centre, University of Pécs.

Mouse Colony helps the lab manage its breeding colony of laboratory mice: it tracks breeding pairs, cages and individual mice, warns when breeders get too old, checks relatedness to avoid inbreeding, predicts offspring genotypes, and keeps a history of procedures and terminations.

The colony records are kept in a Google Sheet owned by the lab. Lab members sign in with their own Google account so the app can read and update that one sheet. Access is controlled by the sheet's normal Google Drive sharing.

The app is used only by members of the lab and is not offered to the public.
